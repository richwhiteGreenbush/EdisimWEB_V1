Chapter 3

Look around

The Look Around menu changes how you see a world. You can shrink to the size of an ant, grow into a giant, fly over the treetops and take photos.

Open it: ☰ Menu then Look Around ▸.

Take a Photo and Fly Around are at the top.

Under them are three sizes: Ant, Me and Giant.

The Time of day slider runs the sun from morning to night.

Your size changes how far your eyes are from the ground. As an ant you're 4 inches tall, as yourself 5 feet, and as a giant 22 feet. As an ant, beside the pond. The geese are giants now.
One bandstand, three sizes
Ant. The path is a road and the bandstand is a cathedral. Me. Back to your own 5 feet. Giant. Twenty-two feet tall: you can see over the roof. Which size makes it easiest to see where the paths go?
Fly Around

Click Fly Around and you lift off the ground. Look up and press ↑ to climb, look down to come back towards the ground. Press Esc to land.

The Park from high above: the gate, the path, the bandstand and the pond, all at once.
Take a Photo

Click Take a Photo. A camera viewfinder appears. Frame your picture by walking and turning, then click to take it.

When the photo appears you have three choices:

  • Hang it here – stand the photo up in the world, right in front of you.
  • Save to my computer – keep it as a picture file.
  • Take another – try again.
A photo of the bandstand, with its three buttons underneath.
Project: Photo safari
  1. Pick one thing in the Park – the bandstand, the gate or the bridge.
  2. Take three photos of it: one as an ant, one as yourself, and one as a giant.
  3. Hang your favourite in the world, right next to the thing it shows.
  4. Save the other two to your computer.
A photo hung in the world. Click it, and you can bring it to life like anything else.

Chapter 4

Visit a world

The Park is just the beginning. The Edusim World Database holds dozens of worlds: dinosaurs, the Moon, a cut-away volcano, a coral reef, the Colosseum and many more.

  1. Open the Menu and click Get More Worlds. The World Database opens in a new tab.
  2. Scroll, or search for a world. Click its picture to read about it.
  3. Click Open this world in Edusim. The world loads, and you arrive at its front door.
The World Database, at edusim3dweb.com/worlds/.
Every world has its own page. The orange button opens it. Opening a world replaces the one you're in. If you've built something you want to keep, use Save World first (Chapter 7).
Go straight there

Every world also has an address of its own. Type this one to go straight to Dinosaur Island:

edusim3dweb.com/app/?world=6
Arriving on Dinosaur Island, at the field station.

Click it, change it

Click almost anything in a world and a small menu opens. It's how you bring things to life, point the camera at them and, later, program them.

Click a park bench and its menu opens beside it.
Bring it to lifeAdds a motion, with no code
CameraPoints your view at it
ProgramOpens the coding blocks (Part 4)
ClosePuts the menu away

A light orb has one more button: Color.

Signs and big boards that say CLICK THIS SIGN TO GO are doors, not objects: clicking one takes you to another world.
Bring it to life
Seven motions, a speed slider, and Hold still to stop.
MotionWhat it doesTry it on
BobFloats gently up and downA balloon
SpinTurns on the spot, foreverA sign
SwayLeans one way, then the otherA tree
BreatheSwells and shrinks, like something aliveAn animal
WobbleJiggles about, never quite stillA jelly

The Speed slider changes how fast a motion goes. Give two trees Sway, one slow and one fast. Which one looks more like a windy day? Motions are saved with your world. Come back tomorrow and the trees will still be swaying.
Light orbs

A light orb is a glowing ball that lights up everything around it. Make one with Create Model ▸ then Load Object ▸ then Light Orb. Click it, choose Color, and pick any colour.

A purple light orb, in front of the bandstand, in the middle of the day.
The time of day

In Look Around, drag the Time of day slider. The sun moves across the sky, the light turns gold and pink, and then it gets dark. Click Back to this world's own time to put it back.

The same orb at sunset. The slider is in the Look Around menu.
Project: A park that moves
  1. Click a tree and choose Bring it to life, then Sway.
  2. Click the geese on the pond and choose Float.
  3. Make a light orb beside the bandstand and give it your favourite colour.
  4. Drag the Time of day slider slowly to sunset and watch the orb take over.
Point the camera

Click anything and choose Camera for four ways to look at it:

Show me thisLifts your view and circles all the way round it
Ride itFloats above it while it moves
See what it seesLooks out of it, facing the way it faces

Press Esc to come back to your own view.

Choose See what it sees on one of the geese. What does the Park look like from the middle of the pond? Down at goose height, the stone bridge looks enormous.

Chapter 6

Draw it, and it inflates

Paint a flat picture, press one button, and it puffs up into a 3D balloon standing in the world, wearing every colour you painted.

  1. Open Create Model ▸ then Load Object ▸ then Draw. A drawing pad opens.
  2. Pick a colour from the row of dots, and set the brush size with the slider.
  3. Paint a big, solid shape. Fill it in – don't just draw an outline.
  4. Add details in other colours: eyes, stripes, spots.
  5. Click Done – Inflate.
A flat painting of a fish, puffed up into a balloon you can walk round.
The drawing pad, with a fish painted in three colours. A thin outline makes a thin, flat balloon. The thicker and more filled-in your painting is, the puffier it gets: the middle of a big shape puffs up the most. Edusim measures how far every painted spot is from the edge of your painting. Spots near the edge stay thin, and spots deep in the middle bulge out the most – on the front and the back at once.

Clear wipes the pad and starts again. Cancel closes it without making anything.

Paint the same shape twice: once as a thin outline, once filled in solid. Inflate both and stand them side by side. Which is puffier?
Project: A balloon zoo

Paint three creatures, one at a time, and inflate each one. They land in front of you, facing you.

A fish, a star and a green blob, all inflated from paintings.
  1. Paint and inflate three different creatures.
  2. Bring them to life: click each one and give it a different motion – Float for the fish, Spin for the star.
  3. Take a photo of your zoo and hang it beside them.
What makes a good balloon?
Big and roundFish, birds, clouds, faces and fruit all puff up beautifully.
Thick armsA star or a starfish works if its points are fat, not thin.
Bold coloursPaint the whole shape first, then add details on top.
Flat thingsSigns, leaves and flags come out like soft pillows.
Paint your own face as a balloon – and stand it next to a balloon of a friend's. Bring yours to life with Watch me, so it turns to follow whoever walks past. A smiley face, painted in four colours and inflated. The Draw tool is a quick, low-stakes way to talk about 2D and 3D: ask students to predict which part of their painting will end up thickest before they press Inflate.

Chapter 7

Save it, share it

Edusim saves your world all by itself – but only in the browser on the computer you're using. To keep a world safe, or send it to somebody else, you turn it into a world file.

Save World
  1. Open the Menu and click Save World.
  2. A file downloads, called something like edusim-world-2026-10-04.json. It holds everything in your world.
  3. Keep it somewhere safe: a USB stick, Google Drive, or an email to yourself.
How a world travels

→
A world fileUSB stick, Drive or email
→
Another computerLoad World File
Save your world now, before you read on. It only takes a click.
Load World File
  1. Open the Menu and click Load World File.
  2. Choose a world file – one you saved, or one somebody sent you.
  3. The world appears, exactly as it was when it was saved, programs and all.
The two orange buttons: Save World and Load World File.

Load World File and opening a world from the database both replace your world. Save first!

Clear World removes everything at once. There is no undo.

 Save World, then Clear World, then Load World File. Is everything back where it was?
Share it with everyone

Anybody can add a world to the World Database. Click Share a world of your own on its front page. You'll need three things:

  • A screenshot of your world, so people can see what it is. (Take a Photo, then Save to my computer.)
  • A description of what you built and why.
  • Your world file, from Save World.
The Share your world page. The Park and Dinosaur Island are in the World Database too, so you can always open a fresh copy of either one. Ask before students share publicly. A world file is also the simplest way to hand in work: students save it and send it to you, and you open it with Load World File and walk around what they made.
Project: World swap

You'll need a friend for this one.

  1. Each make something in your own world: a balloon, a hung photo, a light orb or two.
  2. Save your worlds and swap files.
  3. Load your friend's world. Walk around it and find what they made.
  4. Add one surprise of your own, somewhere they won't expect it.
  5. Save it and send it back. Can they find your surprise?
Anything you make travels inside a world file. A world file is usually small. For most things it doesn't store a picture at all – it stores the recipe for building them again, and Edusim follows the recipe when the file is loaded.
