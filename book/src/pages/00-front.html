How to use this book

This is a book you do. Keep Edusim open beside it, try every step as you read it, and don't worry about going slowly.

Four parts, four colours

Each part has its own colour. Look at the edge of the closed book: a coloured tab on the edge of every page shows where each part starts.

Part 1 · Meet EdusimWhat Edusim is, how to move around, and why building in 3D is good for your brain.
Part 2 · First projectsExplore worlds, take photos, make things move, draw balloons and share your work.
Part 3 · Build itBuild a train, an animal and a train station out of four simple shapes.
Part 4 · Code itProgram the models you built, with drag-and-drop coding blocks.

Each part ends with a skills check: a short list of things to try from memory. Tick them off, and you've earned that part's badge.

Every part uses what the one before it taught. You can jump around, but the projects in Part 4 move the models you build in Part 3.

What you need
  • A computer, a Chromebook or a tablet with a web browser. Chrome works best.
  • The internet, to open Edusim the first time.
  • That's it. There's nothing to download and no account to make.
