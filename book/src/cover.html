Edusim is a free 3D world that runs in a web browser – school Chromebooks included. This book takes you from your very first steps inside it to a whole town you've built and programmed yourself.

Walk through a park, meet a T. rex and paint balloons that puff up into 3D. Then build a steam train, a turtle and a train station from four simple shapes, and bring them to life with drag-and-drop coding blocks: a train that runs to a timetable, an animal that answers back, and street lamps that come on at sunset.

  • 15 chapters of step-by-step projects, from first walk to living town
  • Every picture is a real screenshot, so you can match it on screen
  • Grounded in research on spatial reasoning and 3D learning
  • Challenges, skills checks and notes for teachers
