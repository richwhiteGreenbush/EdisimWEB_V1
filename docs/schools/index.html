The trade-off, said out loud

Edusim has no accounts, so there is no dashboard and no rostering — and no student data is collected, ever. Work saves in the browser and exports as a file. That caps Edusim below the kind of district-wide adoption a gradebook integration is for.

It is also the entire reason a teacher can say yes without asking anyone. Every competing tool in the table above loses at exactly the thirty-second mark, and most of them lose there because of the feature we chose not to build.

If your adoption depends on rostering or a teacher dashboard, Edusim is the wrong tool for that job and we would rather say so here than have you find out in week three.

Trying it with one class

  • 1 · Open it yourself, on a school deviceGo to edusim3dweb.com/app/. You should be standing in a park within seconds. There is no step between that and a class doing the same thing.
  • 2 · Pick one world and one lesson planThe nine plans are a class period each. Dinosaur Island, Ancient Egypt, the Moon and Fantastic Voyage are the ones teachers reach for first.
  • 3 · Send students a linkEvery world has its own address. Put it in your LMS, on the board, or in a bookmark — there is no class code to generate and no seat to assign.
  • 4 · End with Save WorldStudents export a file and hand it in however your school already collects work. On ephemeral devices this is the step that matters.
  • 5 · Tell us what happenedGood or bad. rich.white@greenbush.org — classroom reports are what the next version is built from.
