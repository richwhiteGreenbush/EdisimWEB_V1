📚 The Library

A public reading room with a glass roof, Dewey Decimal aisles, and a card catalog nobody under 30 has ever used.

Getting there: open Edusim, tap ☰ Menu → Load World → The Library. You will be set down at the front gate. Look for the bright ⚡ ACTIVITY boards — there are two of them.

Quick starts — do these first

Both of these are printed on boards inside the world too, so you can leave this card at home. Click the object, choose Program, drag the blocks together in this order, then press Save.

1

Send the book cart back to the stacks

Click the book cart → Program

  1. forever
  2. repeat 15 times
  3. move Z by -1 feet
  4. rotate 180 degrees
  5. repeat 15 times
  6. move Z by 1 feet
  7. rotate 180 degrees

💡 Notice the second loop uses minus one foot. Turning the cart round does not change which way move Z pushes it — you have to flip the number yourself.

2

Give the globe a spin

Click the globe → Program

  1. forever
  2. rotate 1 degrees

💡 The real Earth manages one full turn a day. Find a number that looks convincing rather than dizzying — and remember the globe is tilted, so it wobbles like the real thing.

Then try these three

  1. Cart fleet

    One cart is slow. Duplicate it into a fleet, then send them off — because each copy keeps the program but loses the duplicate block, they will all run the same route without breeding.

    1. repeat 4 times
    2. duplicate 4 ft away

  2. Reading lamp mood

    Program a light orb above the tables to drift between two warm colours. Try to find the pair that actually makes the room feel like a library.

    1. forever
    2. change color to orange
    3. wait 3 seconds
    4. change color to yellow
    5. wait 3 seconds

  3. Shelf on the loose

    Make an entire bookshelf slide out of its row and back again. Librarians would not approve. Time how long one round trip takes, then work out its speed in feet per second.

    1. forever
    2. repeat 10 times
    3. move X by 0.5 feet
    4. wait 0.1 seconds
    5. repeat 10 times
    6. move X by -0.5 feet
    7. wait 0.1 seconds

🤯 Worth knowing

Before computers, every book had a typed card filed in those little drawers — by title, by author, and by subject. Finding one book meant opening three drawers. The Dewey Decimal System that the hanging signs use was invented in 1876 and is still running.

🔨 Build something here

Every world is a place to build in, not just to look at. Open ☰ Menu → Create Model and add something of your own to this one — then click it and program it like everything else. The Model Builder card shows you how, and the tutorials walk you through four projects step by step.

Block colours

  • Control — repeat, forever, wait, duplicate
  • Motion — move X / Y / Z, rotate
  • Looks — change size, change colour

One rule that catches everybody: move X, move Y and move Z slide an object along the world's directions, not the direction it happens to be facing. Turning something does not change which way it will then travel — that is why the loops above go out and come back rather than driving in a circle.
