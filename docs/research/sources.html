Everything the Research Library summarizes, grouped by topic. These are the works cited by the research synthesis this library is built from — external links open the publisher's or repository's own page.

1 · Meta-analyses & effect sizes

  1. Uttal, D. H., et al. (2013). The malleability of spatial skills: A meta-analysis of training studies. Psychological Bulletin, 139(2), 352–402. — 217 studies, g = 0.47.
    pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22663761 · researchgate.net/publication/225184066
  2. Is early spatial skills training effective? A meta-analysis (2020). Frontiers in Psychology. — 20 studies of children aged 0–8, g = 0.96.
    frontiersin.org · fpsyg.2020.01938
  3. Effects of spatial training on mathematics performance: A meta-analysis (2022). — 29 controlled studies, 3,765 participants; math g = 0.28, spatial g = 0.49; 3D and concrete tools transferred best.
    pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35073120 · researchgate.net/publication/358079834
  4. How spatial skills relate to science achievement: A meta-analytic review.
    researchgate.net/publication/408647335
  5. Uttal (2013) full text (PDF) — Current Directions in Psychological Science companion reading.
    idi.ntnu.no · uttal-2013.pdf
  6. Spatial training with blocks and puzzles could unlock mathematical potential. ScienceDaily (2022) — plain-language summary of the math-transfer finding.
    sciencedaily.com/releases/2022/02/220210114114
