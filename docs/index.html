1Edusim

Build it. Code it. Explore it.

A free 3D world in your browser. Stretch simple shapes into anything, program it with blocks or Python-style code, and walk into worlds made for learning.

▶ Launch Edusim — it's free

🔨 Build

Four shapes. Endless models.

Open Menu → Create Model and drop a shape on the ground — a cube, a sphere, a cylinder or a pyramid. A little 🔨 hammer floats over it. The hammer is the whole toolbox: click it and you can stretch the piece, turn it, paint it, and join it to its neighbours.

  1. ✥ Stretch to Shape
    1

    Stretch it into shape

    Choose Stretch to Shape and drag a corner. A cube becomes a wall, a plank or a whole roof — and the opposite corner stays put, so nothing drifts.

    🔄 Rotate/Move Shape adds rings for turning. Turns snap every 15°, so square corners are actually square.

  2. ✥ Move
    2

    Lift it into place

    Drag the green ball above the box to move a piece anywhere — up, down, sideways. That is how a head goes on a body and a roof goes on a house.

  3. 🔗 Nine pieces
    3

    Join the pieces

    Connect to Primitive lists only the pieces touching this one. Tap to join them, and paint each with Apply Texture — a colour, or a picture.

  4. ✨ One model
    4

    Press Render Model

    The hammers vanish and the pile of parts becomes one solid thing you can resize, program, save and copy.

    🆕 Got your own 3D models? Import a .glb file and it gets the hammer too.

🧩 Code

Two ways to write the same program

Click anything in any world and press Program. Start with drag-and-drop blocks. When you are ready, pick Basic Beginner, and your blocks turn into Python-style code you can read and type. Switch back any time. Nothing is ever lost.

Block Code

Snap blocks together

🧩 Program this object

Blocks in four colours: Control (repeat, forever, wait), Motion (move, glide, rotate), Looks (say, size, colour) and Math (numbers you can name). If you have met Scratch, you already know how this works.

forever move forward 10 feet rotate 90 degrees
Python-style code

Or type it

BlocksBasic BeginnerBasic Pro 
# the same program, typed
forever
    move forward 10 feet
    rotate 90 degrees

The exact same program, typed. Basic Beginner reads just like the blocks. Basic Pro adds the Python habits: text in quotes, = and ==. If a line has a problem, a card says what to change, and Fix it can change it for you.

▶ Every programmed object gets a green play button floating above it. Programs save with the world and start themselves the next time you open it.

2Edusim Stages 🎭 New · Edusim Stages

Make games, stories and animations

Edusim Stages is a stage, sprites, backdrops and blocks, laid out the way anyone who has used Scratch will recognise. Then it does the Edusim things: any of the Model Database's 3D models becomes a sprite you can turn round, Edusim's worlds become backdrops, every script can be read and written as text, and every project can be saved as a video. And when it is ready, 🌍 Convert to 3D World stands it up in Edusim, still running, for you to walk into and play in 3D.

3Edusim HiFi ✦ New · Edusim HiFi

The same worlds, in high fidelity

Edusim HiFi is built for higher-end computers and a richer visual experience: a living sky that lights everything under it, real shadows, grass and ferns you walk through, photographic stone, timber and metal, and models rebuilt in far finer detail. Everything else is the Edusim you know — every world, the block and Python-style coding, Create Model, Draw, saving and sharing.

  • 🌤️ A real sky, from noon to starlight
  • 🌿 Grass, ferns and trees in leaf
  • 🪨 Photographic stone, wood and metal
  • 🌗 Soft shadows and lamps that come on at dusk

🍎 Parents & teachers

Made for Learning

And built on research-based practices — not just kid-tested, but study-backed.

Edusim is the only 3D building-and-coding platform a K–8 teacher can start in thirty seconds — no account, no install, no IT ticket, no cost — with curricular worlds ready to explore on day one.

🔬 Research-based practices

A research-based learning tool for kids

Edusim is built on one of the strongest findings in learning science: spatial reasoning — picturing, turning and building things in your mind — predicts success in science and math for decades, it's highly trainable, and it grows fastest in hands-on 3D environments with coding. That's not our hunch; it's what meta-analyses spanning hundreds of studies measured. Our Research Library walks through the evidence in plain language and shows how each finding became an Edusim feature.

What Edusim deliberately does not do

There are no accounts, so there is no teacher dashboard, no rostering and no cross-device save — and no student data is collected, ever. Students save work in the browser and hand it in as a file.

That is the whole trade. It is why there is nothing for a technology department to approve, nothing to purchase, and no consent form — and why a teacher can use it tomorrow morning without asking anyone.
