✨ A 3D World You Build Yourself

Imagine, Create, Share your world!

Edusim: Web Edition is a giant 3D playground that lives right in your web browser. Start in a huge city park, then travel to an art museum, a public library, the surface of the Moon, a base on Mars, an island of full-size dinosaurs, or the inside of the human body — and make every one of them your own. Import 3D models, paint a squiggle and watch it puff into a balloon, drop glowing light orbs, teach your creations to move with drag-and-drop blocks, and step into any of it with a VR headset.

Explore the Worlds - Change the Worlds

You don't start on an empty field. Open Menu → Load World and step straight into a park, a museum, a library, the Moon, a base on Mars, an island of full-size dinosaurs, or a walk-through tour of the human body (and more are being added). Little signs all over each one tell you what you're looking at, and you can move, resize, or program absolutely anything you find.

Hop between worlds any time

Tap ☰ Menu, then Load World, and pick one. The whole world swaps around you — new ground, new sky, new everything — and you're set down right at its front gate. Everything you can add to a world lives under Load Object, so the menu stays short however much goes into it.

💾 Your changes save themselves as you go, so a world is exactly how you left it next time. Picking it from the menu again gives you a fresh copy to start over with.

🥽 View in VR

Step inside any world with a VR headset

Open Menu → VR Headset View and the world splits into two pictures, one for each eye. Put a headset on and you're standing in it — look around by turning your head, and walk with the controllers. The menu comes with you as a panel floating right there beside you, so you can hop to another world without ever taking the headset off.

🥽

With a real headset

Browse to Edusim on the headset itself and pick VR Headset View. Head tracking, walking on the thumbsticks, and point-and-click on the in-world menu with a controller.

📱

With a phone

No headset needed. Slide a phone into a cardboard-style viewer and it uses the phone's own motion sensors to look around. Choose things by resting your gaze on them for a second.

💻

On a plain laptop

It still works — you get the two-eye view full screen, which is a neat way to see how VR actually works. Press Esc whenever you want to come back.

🛟 A few things — importing files, the drawing pad, and web panels — need a normal screen, so picking those drops you back out of VR first. Everything else, including all the worlds, works without leaving the headset.

Bring In Anything

Import your own 3D models & pictures

Tap Import and drag in a 3D model, a photo, or even a silly animated GIF — Edusim automatically resizes it to fit perfectly in your world. Grab free models and textures from the built-in "Get free 3D models" link and start decorating right away.

  • ✓ Works with .glb, .gltf, .obj, .png, .jpg, and animated .gif files
  • ✓ Everything is auto-scaled so nothing looks too big or too tiny
  • ✓ Just drag a file straight onto the window

Pure Imagination

Paint it, then... POOF! It's 3D!

Pick a color and a brush size and paint absolutely anything — a face, a star, a blob monster, whatever you dream up. Hit Done — Inflate and your flat painting puffs up into a round, puffy 3D balloon, textured in your very own colors.

  • ✓ Paint with as many colors and strokes as you like

💡 Light Orb
✨ Real glowing light
Light Up the Night

Drop glowing light orbs anywhere

One tap of Light Orb drops a glowing sphere of light that actually lights up the ground around it — not just a picture of glowing, real light! Every click cycles to a new color, and you can fine-tune the exact glow from the orb's own Color menu.

  • ✓ Casts real light onto the hills and grass nearby
  • ✓ Cycles through a rainbow of colors as you place more
  • ✓ Great for lighting up a nighttime scene or a secret hideout
🧩 Program this object
🧠 No coding experience needed
Coding, Made Playful

Teach your creations new tricks

Click anything and tap Program to open a friendly, drag-and-drop block editor — just like Scratch. Snap together blocks like "repeat 10 times," "rotate 15 degrees," and "change color to..." to build spinning sculptures, bouncing balloons, or slow-glowing orbs.

  • ✓ Drag colorful blocks together — no typing required
  • ✓ A green ▶ play icon floats above anything you've programmed
  • ✓ Great first taste of real programming logic and loops
🌐 Web Browser
📖 Real, live web pages
Even More Magic

Bring a real web page into your world

Place a real, clickable web browser panel right in your 3D world — complete with its own address bar. It opens on Edusim's own Wikipedia page to get you started, so you can build a little reading nook, a research corner, or just park a fun page next to your treehouse.

  • ✓ Type any address into the panel's own toolbar
  • ✓ Click, scroll, and type on the page just like normal
  • ✓ Tap the little ✎ icon above it to resize or move the panel
👆 Object menu
Everything Is Yours to Change

Tap anything to make it yours

Nothing in your world is stuck the way it started. Click any object to pop open its menu — resize it bigger or smaller, nudge it around, or jump straight into programming it. Changes save automatically, so everything is exactly how you left it next time you visit.

Works on a laptop, or a tablet on the couch

On a computer, walk with the arrow keys and click-drag to look around. On a tablet or touchscreen, a big friendly on-screen D-pad appears in the corner — press and hold to walk and turn, with room for more than one finger at once.

For Parents & Teachers

Built for imagination, not for data collection

Edusim: Web Edition runs entirely in the browser — there's no server watching, no account to create, and no one else to run into.

🔒

No accounts, no strangers

This is a single-player sandbox. There's no chat, no multiplayer, and no login — just your child and their world.

💾

Saves itself, right on the device

Everything is stored locally in the browser automatically. "Save World" and "Load World" let you back up or share a creation as a file.

🚫

No ads, no purchases

There's nothing to buy and nothing to click by accident. It's just a creative space to build in.

🧠

Real skills, playfully taught

Spatial reasoning, color and design, and an honest first taste of programming logic through drag-and-drop blocks.

🪧

Worlds that quietly teach

Every ready-made world is dotted with short, readable signs — park design and geology, art movements, the Dewey Decimal System, the Apollo landings, what it would take to live on Mars, life in the late Cretaceous, and how your own organs fit together. Written for ages 12–15, and easy to ignore if a child just wants to build.

🖥️

Runs on what you already have

It's a web app — no installs, no app store, no special hardware. Just open it in a modern browser.

🌐

Heads up on web panels

The optional Web Browser panel can show real pages like Wikipedia — worth a quick look together the first time.

</> For Developers

The whole thing is open — go make your own version

Edusim: Web Edition is a single-page Three.js app with no backend and no build magic. Every world you just read about is generated in plain JavaScript, so there's nothing hidden: read it, change it, and make it yours.

Fork it, branch it, make it yours

Clone the repository, cut yourself a branch, and start changing things. Add a world of your own to src/props/, teach the block editor a new block, restyle the whole place — it's your copy, and you can't break anyone else's.

  • No backend. Pure client-side Three.js that ships as a static dist/ folder — host it anywhere.
  • Worlds are code. Each prebuilt world is a list of records and a set of prop builders, so a new one is a new file plus one entry in a table.
  • Read CLAUDE.md first. It's the architecture tour, written as the project was built — including the traps that were hit along the way.
